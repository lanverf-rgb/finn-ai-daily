## 2. EU AI Office Issues New Compliance Guidelines for Open-Source Foundation Models
**What happened:** The European Union AI Office published its finalized compliance framework for open-source AI developers, clarifying that models made freely available to the public are exempt from most high-risk obligations unless they exceed specific compute thresholds. The guidance aims to prevent a developer exodus while maintaining strict safety standards for frontier systems.
**Why it matters:** It provides legal clarity for the European open-source AI ecosystem, which had threatened to relocate operations over regulatory ambiguity.
**Source:** Reuters

## 3. Anthropic Expands Claude's Autonomous Agent Capabilities to Enterprise Software
**What happened:** Anthropic has launched new computer-use capabilities for Claude, enabling the assistant to natively interact with complex enterprise software suites like Salesforce, SAP, and Workday via standard graphical user interfaces. The system uses visual processing to navigate legacy applications that lack modern APIs.
**Why it matters:** It drastically broadens the scope of enterprise automation by bridging the gap between modern AI and legacy software infrastructure.
**Source:** VentureBeat

## 4. Google DeepMind Unveils WeatherBench 3 for Sub-Kilometer Climate Prediction
**What happened:** Google DeepMind researchers released WeatherBench 3, an upgraded machine learning benchmark and operational forecasting model capable of predicting localized weather patterns at a sub-kilometer resolution up to 10 days in advance. The model processes petabytes of satellite and sensor data in a fraction of the time required by traditional numerical weather prediction supercomputers.
**Why it matters:** High-resolution hyper-local forecasting is critical for disaster management and optimizing renewable energy grids.
**Source:** Nature
