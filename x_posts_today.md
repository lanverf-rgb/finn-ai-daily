## 1. OpenAI Releases GPT-5.1 with Advanced Long-Context Reasoning
**What happened:** OpenAI has officially rolled out GPT-5.1, featuring a native 2-million-token context window and significantly reduced hallucination rates in complex coding tasks. The update also introduces more granular control over agentic workflows directly within the API.
**Why it matters:** It sets a new benchmark for enterprise applications requiring the synthesis of massive, multi-document codebases and financial records.
**Source:** TechCrunch

## 2. EU Enforces Strict New Transparency Rules for Foundation Models
**What happened:** The European Union has begun enforcing its latest compliance framework, requiring all developers of foundational AI models with more than 10^25 FLOPs to disclose detailed training data summaries and copyright compliance audits. Non-compliant providers face fines of up to 7% of global annual turnover.
**Why it matters:** This establishes the strictest global baseline yet for AI copyright enforcement and data provenance.
**Source:** Reuters

## 3. Google DeepMind Unveils AlphaFold 4 for Real-Time Molecular Dynamics
**What happened:** Google DeepMind announced AlphaFold 4, which can now predict not just static protein structures, but continuous molecular interactions and binding dynamics in real time. The tool is being made immediately available to academic researchers worldwide.
**Why it matters:** This breakthrough drastically accelerates drug discovery pipelines by moving from structural prediction to simulating biological function on the fly.
**Source:** Nature

## 4. Meta Open-Sources Llama 4 Code-Optimized Variants
**What happened:** Meta has released the Llama 4 Code family of open-weights models, ranging from 8B to 70B parameters, trained specifically on multi-language programming and system architecture tasks. Developers can run and fine-tune the models locally
